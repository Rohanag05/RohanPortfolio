Hi! I’m Rohan Agarwal, a current undergraduate studying Data Science and Computer Science at the University of California, Berkeley.

I’m interested in machine learning and software, particularly building intelligent systems that can learn, reason, and work autonomously. Recently, I built an ingredient scanner that uses OCR and the Open Food Facts API to identify ingredients and recommend healthier alternatives, as well as a Pacman agent using graph search and multi-agent algorithms like A*, minimax, and expectimax. I’m interested in applying these ideas to agentic AI, reinforcement learning, NLP, and deep learning.
